# RigShade Website — Master Context

**Document type:** Master website context
**Project:** RigShade
**Status:** Active
**Website baseline:** v1.10
**App reference:** RigShade 1.0.1
**Last update:** 2026-09-26

## 1. Purpose

This file is the reference for the RigShade website. It records the decisions that are already
approved so that a later change does not undo them, and it wins when a brief is ambiguous. It
describes the website only.

## 2. What the site is for

RigShade is an Android USB OTG editor and preset notebook for the M-VAVE Cube Baby electric guitar
version. The site talks to musicians: short, clear, practical. It presents the app, links to the
privacy policy and answers support questions.

## 3. The name

The product is **RigShade**. No earlier working name may appear in copy, metadata, page titles, alt
text, navigation, image captions, shop text or in any document meant for publication.

## 4. Visual identity

- Background: solid Navy `#082B55`, with darker Navy panels `#061F42` for hierarchy. No gradients,
  glow, noise, texture, light spots or background photography.
- Type: solid white, with light grey for secondary text. No coloured or gradient text. Accents are
  for icons, indicators, selected states and controls, never as decoration.
- Accents: mint `#27E5B1`, sky `#33B8FF`, violet `#7A5CFF`. Nothing outside this palette.
- Rounded controls, generous spacing, flat surfaces. The site should feel related to the app
  without copying the app interface.

## 5. Logo and favicon

- Use the approved v1.4 mark: one exterior frame and three internal blocks, with no connector lines
  and no arrow. Earlier versions do not come back.
- The favicon is three rounded vertical bars on a transparent background, in green, blue then
  violet order, with no border, no square behind it, no glow and no shadow.
- Both live in `assets/brand/`.

## 6. Screenshots

- Only real captures of the app, kept in `assets/screenshots/`.
- Portrait and landscape are always presented as two separate contexts, each with its own heading,
  its own text and its own container. They are never merged into one card: that reads as if the
  portrait screen contained a horizontal interface.

## 7. Pages

`/` the product page, `/privacy/` the privacy policy, `/support/` help and answers. The product page
keeps its sections: header, hero, the four features (USB OTG, Cube Baby guitar, preset banks, IR
workflow), the app showcase in both orientations, the closing benefits and the footer, which links
to Support and Privacy.

## 8. Behaviour

- Desktop, phone portrait and phone landscape all work; none of them is an afterthought.
- Desktop and rotated phones favour the wide captures; portrait stacks and shows the mobile captures
  first.
- The mobile menu button is solid white at full opacity, and its expanded state stays in step with
  the menu.
- Accessible by default: headings in order, meaningful alt text, visible focus, enough contrast, and
  no information carried by colour alone.

## 9. Wording

- Compatibility is exactly: Android + USB OTG + the M-VAVE Cube Baby electric guitar version. No
  wider claim until it is true.
- No invented shop link: the Google Play button leads to the support page until the listing is
  public.
- Keep engineering vocabulary out of public copy.

## 10. Metadata

Title `RigShade — Shape Your Sound`, theme colour `#082B55`, `lang="en"`. No earlier name in any
title or description.

## 11. What may change, and what may not

Copy, spacing, responsive fixes, accessibility, metadata, asset tidying and newer real screenshots
may change freely. The name, the logo construction, the navy palette, the favicon, the flat look,
the compatibility claim and the page structure may not, unless the request says so.
